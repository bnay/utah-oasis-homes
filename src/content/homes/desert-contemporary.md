Framed by the red-rock landscape of Southern Utah, this contemporary home brings together low-slung geometry, warm natural materials, and sheltered outdoor spaces. Walls of glass open the refined interior to a private courtyard, pool, and outdoor kitchen, creating an effortless connection between daily life and the desert setting.
