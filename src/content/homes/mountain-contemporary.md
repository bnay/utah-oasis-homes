Set against the Wasatch foothills, this mountain contemporary home pairs angular rooflines, stone, and warm wood with an interior shaped by natural light and clean architectural lines. Expansive glass connects the vaulted great room and open kitchen to the landscape, while tailored cabinetry, sculptural lighting, and a serene primary suite soften the modern design with warmth and refinement.
