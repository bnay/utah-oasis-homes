Set beneath the Wasatch mountains, this welcoming home blends modern farmhouse character with spaces designed for gathering. Warm hardwood, soft blue cabinetry, and generous windows give the interior an easy sense of comfort, while the covered patio, landscaped fire-pit terrace, lower-level family room, and theater make room for every part of family life.
