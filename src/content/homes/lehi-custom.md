This refined Lehi home was shaped around the rhythms of modern family life, with a soaring great room, warm natural finishes, and gathering spaces that feel open, comfortable, and quietly polished. An upstairs loft, dedicated theater, and indoor basketball court bring recreation into the heart of the design, creating a home built to serve its family beautifully for years to come.
